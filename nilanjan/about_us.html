About Nilanjan Guest House

30 Years. 78 Rooms. A Grand Heritage Legacy

We present to you a unique mix of heritage with the comforts of contemporary furnishing and facilities at each of our 78 rooms. Located on prestigious Chowringhee Road near Exide Crossing and Rabindra Sadan Metro Station, Nilanjan Guest House offers serene solitude amidst the bustling heart of Kolkata.

Whether you are visiting for an intellectual getaway, business conference, family trip, or medical travel, our heritage building promises unparalleled comfort, convenience, and attentive room service.
