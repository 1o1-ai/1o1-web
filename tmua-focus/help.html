Common questions

Can I try it before registering?

Yes. Choose a fictional student in the Student menu to explore their sample coaching profile, or use My practice to start with a blank profile.

What do the IQ-style and EQ-style activities tell me?

The reasoning baseline shows how you did on a short set of mathematical and logic questions. The check-in records your preferences for feedback, pressure and motivation. Both guide study. They do not produce standardised IQ or EQ scores.

What if I leave an assessment halfway through?

Use Save and exit to return later. The reasoning timer keeps running while you are away. The EQ check-in is untimed, and you can skip any question.

Where is my progress saved?

Profiles, answers and notes are saved in this browser. Return on the same device and browser to see your progress, and select the same student.
