        How this demo calculates the decision

        The engine evaluates 400 fixed, repeatable scenarios across two customer tanks, two plants, and three dispatch choices. Scenarios share demand shocks across sites and vary current inventory, consumption and travel time. A simulated threshold breach occurs when projected inventory drops below the configured reorder reserve before arrival. Distance, cost, travel time and capacity are illustrative inputs.

        Rules reject any plan that violates the confirmed receiving window or depletes available plant inventory below its modeled reserve. Among plans meeting the risk limit, the engine prioritizes preserving confirmed stops and then minimizing additional cost. If no plan qualifies, it escalates to the dispatcher.

        Deployment concept: private OrgBrain inference on H200-class infrastructure, statistical validation with subject-matter experts, and a separate AES/SSH transport architecture. This browser demo runs its scenario model locally using synthetic data.
