Working in Germany

Berlin and regional cities

Destination strengths

Industrial regions and strong local identities

Germany gives engineering and trades candidates industrial settings to explore, alongside hospitality, logistics and healthcare. Berlin, an industrial town and a regional city can offer very different rents, commutes and language demands.

Three places to explore

Berlin’s Museum Island

Visit the collections and walk along the Spree in the heart of the capital.

Rhine Valley

Plan a weekend among riverside towns, vineyards and hilltop castles.

Munich and Bavaria

Explore city museums, regional food and day trips toward the Alps.

Culture and everyday life

Regional customs and food vary widely. Practise the German you will actually use at work, on the train and when arranging housing.
