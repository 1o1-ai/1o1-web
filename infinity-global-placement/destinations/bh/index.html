Working in Bahrain

Manama

Destination strengths

Island scale, layers of history

Bahrain combines a compact urban setting with port logistics, hospitality and industrial activity. Manama and Muharraq offer markedly different ways to spend time after work.

Three places to explore

The Pearling Path

Walk through Muharraq and learn how the pearl trade shaped its homes and streets.

Bahrain Fort

Explore an archaeological site associated with the ancient Dilmun civilisation.

Manama Souq

Browse traditional shops near Bab Al Bahrain and make time for local food.

Culture and everyday life

Start with Muharraq’s old streets and the stories of the pearling families who lived there. Weekend trips can be short enough to fit around a changing roster.
