Working in Singapore

Singapore

Destination strengths

City convenience, neighbourhood character

Singapore combines aviation, maritime logistics, advanced manufacturing, healthcare and hospitality in one city-state. Walk a potential commute at the time you would finish work and compare room rents near useful transit lines.

Three places to explore

Marina Bay and Gardens by the Bay

See the waterfront and the Supertree Grove, or make a longer visit to the conservatories.

Kampong Gelam and Little India

Explore independent shops, food and the history of two distinct neighbourhoods.

Hawker centres

Start with a meal near your neighbourhood and discover how much of Singapore’s daily life happens at shared tables.

Culture and everyday life

Food gives you an easy way into the city’s Malay, Chinese, Indian and Peranakan traditions. Try a neighbourhood hawker centre as well as the landmark districts.
