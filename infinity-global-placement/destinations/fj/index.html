Working in Fiji

Suva and beyond

Destination strengths

Island life varies by assignment

Suva offers city life and public institutions, while the Coral Coast and offshore islands are more closely associated with resorts and tourism. The island you work on shapes your commute, housing options and access to services.

Three places to explore

Suva

Explore the capital’s markets and the Fiji Museum to learn about the country’s history.

Coral Coast

Set aside a day for beaches and coastal communities on Viti Levu.

Mamanuca Islands

Plan a boat trip and check return times before committing to a day out.

Culture and everyday life

Fiji’s communities include iTaukei and Indo-Fijian traditions. Follow your host’s guidance on village etiquette and make time to explore local markets and food.
