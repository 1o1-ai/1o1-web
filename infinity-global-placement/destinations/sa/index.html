Working in Saudi Arabia

Riyadh and Jeddah

Destination strengths

One country, very different cities

Riyadh offers major institutions and urban development, while Jeddah has a Red Sea setting and a long trading history. For engineering, healthcare and hospitality candidates, the location of the assignment matters as much as the employer’s name.

Three places to explore

AlUla and Hegra

Allow time for an overnight trip to see sandstone landscapes and the Nabataean tombs of Hegra.

Diriyah

Explore the historic mud-brick architecture at At-Turaif near Riyadh.

Historic Jeddah

Walk through Al-Balad and look for the timber balconies and old trading streets.

Culture and everyday life

Riyadh and Jeddah offer distinct routines. Explore local cafés, heritage districts and seasonal events. Ask colleagues about local customs and how opening hours change during Ramadan.
