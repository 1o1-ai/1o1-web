Working in Malaysia

Kuala Lumpur and Penang

Destination strengths

A choice of cities and coastlines

Kuala Lumpur has metropolitan hospitality and services. Penang combines industrial activity with a distinctive food and heritage scene, while Melaka offers a quieter historic setting for a short break.

Three places to explore

Kuala Lumpur

See the Petronas Twin Towers and spend time in the city’s food markets and neighbourhoods.

George Town, Penang

Explore shophouses, clan jetties and hawker food on foot.

Melaka

Follow the river through the historic centre and look for traces of the city’s trading history.

Culture and everyday life

Get to know the country through Malay, Chinese, Indian and Peranakan food and neighbourhoods. Compare housing and shift transport in the city where the actual role is based.
