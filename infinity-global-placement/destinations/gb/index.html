Working in United Kingdom

London and regional towns

Destination strengths

Several nations and many local job markets

The UK offers opportunities to explore across healthcare, hospitality, engineering and distribution. A London salary or commute tells you little about life in a regional town, so compare the location attached to the offer.

Three places to explore

London’s South Bank

Walk along the Thames and combine galleries, markets and riverside landmarks.

Edinburgh’s Old Town

Explore the Royal Mile and the city’s closes, then make time for a hill walk if the weather allows.

Lake District

Plan a longer weekend around lakeside towns and walking routes suited to the conditions.

Culture and everyday life

Explore regional museums, markets and food rather than treating the UK as a single culture. Ask where the worksite is and what the early-morning or late-night trip will look like.
