Working in United Arab Emirates

Dubai and Abu Dhabi

Destination strengths

Seven emirates, many ways to live

Dubai’s international hotels, airports and trade networks sit alongside Abu Dhabi’s cultural institutions and healthcare centres. The emirate and neighbourhood you choose can change your commute and weekend routine considerably.

Three places to explore

Al Fahidi and Dubai Creek

Cross the creek by abra and spend an afternoon exploring historic lanes and souqs.

Burj Khalifa and Downtown Dubai

See the modern skyline from Downtown and explore the area on foot as the weather cools.

Hatta

Head inland for a mountain weekend and explore a different side of Dubai.

Culture and everyday life

Look beyond the skyline. Emirati hospitality, coffee and shared meals are part of everyday social life. Explore Ramadan schedules with your team and plan outdoor outings around summer heat.
