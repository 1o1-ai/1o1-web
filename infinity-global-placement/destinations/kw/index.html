Working in Kuwait

Kuwait City

Destination strengths

Waterfront life and a strong trading heritage

Kuwait City combines retail and distribution, healthcare, industrial services and coastal neighbourhoods. A realistic offer comparison starts with the distance between your worksite and the housing you can afford.

Three places to explore

Kuwait Towers

Use the waterfront landmark as a starting point for exploring the city’s coast.

Souq Al-Mubarakiya

Browse a traditional market and stop for a meal among its shops and cafés.

The Scientific Center

Visit the aquarium and waterfront in Salmiya on a day off.

Culture and everyday life

Markets and family gatherings offer a closer look at life beyond the malls. Kuwait’s seafaring and trading heritage is visible in its waterfront and cultural institutions.
