Working in Malta

Valletta and nearby towns

Destination strengths

Mediterranean heritage on a small scale

Malta brings hospitality, port services, facilities and healthcare close together. Housing availability, traffic and the trip between islands can make a short distance on a map feel longer.

Three places to explore

Valletta

Explore the fortified capital’s streets and viewpoints over the Grand Harbour.

Mdina

Walk through the old walled city and explore nearby Rabat.

Gozo

Allow a full day for the ferry, the Citadel in Victoria and the island’s coastal landscape.

Culture and everyday life

Look for village festas and neighbourhood bakeries to see everyday Maltese culture. Ask which language is used with customers or patients in your specific workplace.
