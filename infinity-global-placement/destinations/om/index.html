Working in Oman

Muscat

Destination strengths

From Muscat’s coast to mountain towns

Oman’s ports, tourism, utilities and industrial sites span very different settings. A role based in Muscat can offer a different routine from work near a port or inland facility.

Three places to explore

Muttrah Corniche and Souq

Pair a waterfront walk with a visit to the market for frankincense and crafts.

Nizwa Fort and Souq

Spend a day inland exploring the fort and market streets.

Jabal Akhdar

Plan a mountain trip for terrace landscapes and cooler air.

Culture and everyday life

Explore Omani food, coffee and frankincense traditions through markets rather than rushing between landmarks. Plan transport in advance for days out beyond Muscat.
