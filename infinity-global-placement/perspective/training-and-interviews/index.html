Global Perspective

From your first call to your first interview

How Infinity helps you prepare for the role and the employer.

Start with the role you actually want

Infinity describes assessing candidates’ skills and experience before matching them with suitable roles. Bring examples of the work you do well and name the settings you want to explore. A hotel applicant might highlight guest service and complaints handling, while an engineering applicant might bring examples of equipment, safety procedures or installations.

Build your interview toolkit

Infinity’s training page describes CV preparation, mock interviews, skills development and language training. Ask which modules apply to your role. Practise two or three examples: what happened, what you did and what changed as a result.

Use the interview to learn about the employer

Ask about the specific worksite, supervisor, roster, onboarding and how success is measured in the first month. After the interview, confirm with Infinity who will update you and what documents or follow-up the employer expects.
