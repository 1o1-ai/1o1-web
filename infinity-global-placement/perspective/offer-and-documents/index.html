From job offer to departure

How Infinity helps candidates prepare documentation and review practical relocation details.

Read the written offer together

Infinity says its team explains employer benefits before candidates accept. Compare the job title, worksite, base pay, overtime, deductions and any promised housing, meals or transport. Ask the recruiter to clarify each item that is unclear and identify who provides it.

Build a document checklist for your case

Infinity describes visa and documentation guidance. Ask for the exact checklist for your role and country, the entity responsible for each step, and how you will receive updates. Organise copies of documents and record who has the originals.

Prepare for the departure briefing

Use the orientation to confirm your itinerary, arrival contact and first reporting address. Ask what to do if a flight changes, a pickup falls through or your accommodation differs from the written arrangement.
