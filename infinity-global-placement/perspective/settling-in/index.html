Your first month abroad

How pre-departure preparation and post-placement contact can help you establish a routine.

Before you board

Infinity describes pre-departure orientation covering local culture, emergency contacts and accommodation guidance. Confirm who meets you, where you are staying, how you get to your first shift and which contact works outside office hours.

During your first week

Ask your employer for the onboarding schedule and a named supervisor. Map your commute at shift change, learn where to buy essentials and budget for costs before your first payday. Keep the recruiter and employer contact details accessible offline.

If something needs attention

Infinity lists post-placement support among its services. Ask before departure who handles questions after arrival, what support covers and how to escalate a discrepancy in your schedule, housing or assigned duties. Keep a written record of what was agreed and share it with the appropriate contact.
