OrgBrain

OrgBrain is the blank-slate business brain Brahmexa is building: a system that connects to ERP, HR, payroll, finance, CRM, documents, and workflow tools, then learns the enterprise from every approved transaction.

How it learns Brahmexa
OBbusiness memory

Enterprise memory

Appointment letters, invoices, approvals, policies, contracts, messages, and system events become governed records, not loose files.

Learning engine

OrgBrain does not train on everything blindly. It converts transactions into structured signals, patterns, and suggested policies.

Nexus interface

Nexus talks to people. OrgBrain remembers the business. Agents act only through policy, permissions, and audit trails.

How the business is learned

Every transaction becomes a signal.

The first version focuses on observing, indexing, and explaining the organization before taking over responsibilities. Human approval is the bridge from detected pattern to active rule.

IngestERP, HR, payroll, CRM, finance, documents, chat, email, and workflow events.
NormalizeTurn raw activity into business events with actors, dates, amounts, objects, and source proof.
ResolveConnect people, departments, vendors, customers, documents, and transactions into an entity graph.
DetectFind repeated approvals, exceptions, bottlenecks, risks, preferences, and hidden workflows.
SuggestGenerate explainable rules, automations, and next-best actions for review.
GovernApproved rules become operating memory with versioning, policy checks, and audit.
Architecture principle

RAG remembers. Learning Engine learns.

LLMs help read, reason, explain, and draft. RAG retrieves evidence. The learning engine builds durable business memory from structured events, relationships, policies, and feedback.

OrgBrain v0 promise

Watch first. Learn safely. Suggest with evidence. Act only after permission. Replace legacy systems phase by phase, never by surprise.
