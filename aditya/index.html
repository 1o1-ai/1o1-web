SURFACE ENGINEERING DIVISION

Liquid Carbo Nitriding (LCN) & Manganese Phosphating

BLW Engine Valves operates state-of-the-art salt bath nitriding lines and automatic phosphating tanks designed to dramatically enhance component durability, wear resistance, and thermal fatigue resistance under high operating RPMs.

Liquid Carbo Nitriding (LCN / NOPQ / QPQ)

Creates a non-brittle compound layer with exceptional wear resistance, low friction coefficient, and 96+ hour salt spray corrosion defense.

9-Tank Automatic Manganese Phosphating

Produces a crystalline manganese iron phosphate coating that absorbs engine oil, prevents metal-to-metal galling during initial engine break-in, and ensures anti-fretting.

High Temperature Metallurgy

Engineered for temperatures up to 800°C using premium materials such as SUH3 (Austenitic Stainless Steel), 21-4N (EV8), and martensitic steel with hard-faced Stellite alloys.

Microstructure Comparison & Performance

Standard Treated Valve
  • High surface friction coefficient
  • Susceptible to stem galling at high RPM
  • Lower fatigue limit (~350 MPa)
BLW Liquid Nitrided (LCN) Valve
  • Ultra-low friction (0.08 μ)
  • Zero scuffing & stem seizure defense
  • High fatigue limit (> 650 MPa)
  • 850-1000 HV surface hardness

Salt Bath Nitriding Workflow

1 Degrease & Wash
2 Pre-heat (350°C)
3 LCN Bath (570°C)
4 Quench & Polish
