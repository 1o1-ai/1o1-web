1. Executive Summary & Website Audit Findings

Our technical team performed a thorough analysis of BLW's current online portal at https://blwenginevalves.com/. While BLW's 50+ year manufacturing legacy and IATF 16949 credentials are exceptionally strong, several friction points slow down B2B buyers:

Key Audit Evidence Items Identified:

Hardcoded Template Address Artifact: The current website header displays 304 North Cardinal St. Dorchester Center, MA 02124 (a generic template placeholder) alongside the real Bahadurgarh, Haryana address, causing customer confusion.
Missing RFQ Basket System: Overseas buyers purchasing multiple SKUs (e.g. Pulsar 220 valves + Apache guides) cannot combine parts into a single inquiry; they must re-submit separate contact forms for every single SKU.
